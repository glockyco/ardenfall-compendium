## Process notes

- **Plan amendments are commits, not edits-in-place-without-record.** Every plan deviation discovered during execution lands as a `fix(plan):` commit before the implementer dispatch runs.
- **Two-stage review per task** when the work has substantive content (logic, configs with semantics). For purely declarative bootstraps that the controller can self-verify, spec compliance is controller-performed and only code quality is delegated.
- **IRC is durable.** When an implementer DMs the controller, the exchange persists in their report and in the controller's per-turn IRC injection. Re-read implementer reports carefully before doubting them.
